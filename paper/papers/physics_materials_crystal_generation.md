# Physics & Materials Science — Symmetry, Crystal Generation, High-Entropy Alloys

## [1] Space Group Informed Transformer for Crystalline Materials Generation
- **Authors:** Multiple
- **Venue:** arXiv / ICLR 2024
- **Year:** 2024
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.15734
- **Abstract:** CrystalFormer generates crystals by sampling Wyckoff descriptors conditioned on space group. Space group symmetry reduces crystal space, enabling data-efficient generative modeling.
- **PAGC Relevance:** Space groups are finite combinatorial structures (230 distinct groups) that constrain all possible crystal geometries — exactly as PAGC's 27×8 matrix constrains all possible token sequences. The Wyckoff position ↔ base×modifier mapping is structurally homologous.

## [2] SymmCD: Symmetry-Preserving Crystal Generation with Diffusion Models
- **Authors:** Multiple
- **Venue:** arXiv
- **Year:** 2025
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.03638
- **Abstract:** Decomposes crystals into asymmetric unit + symmetry transformations. Novel interpretable representation enables generalization across crystallographic symmetry groups.
- **PAGC Relevance:** PAGC's 27 bases = asymmetric unit; 8 modifiers = symmetry operations. The crystal structure is regenerated by applying modifiers to bases — the same generative logic as crystallographic space group generation.

## [3] CrystalFlow: A Flow-Based Generative Model for Crystalline Materials
- **Authors:** Multiple
- **Venue:** Nature Communications
- **Year:** 2025
- **URL:** https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-64364-4
- **Abstract:** Combines Continuous Normalizing Flows and Conditional Flow Matching with graph-based equivariant neural networks. Symmetry-aware representations for lattice parameters, atomic coordinates, and atom types.
- **PAGC Relevance:** The three-channel representation (lattice + coordinates + atom types) mirrors PAGC's three-layer structure (base × modifier × lexical cache). Flow-based generation from structured priors validates PAGC's combinatorial approach.
