# Methods: Linguistics, Generative Grammar & African Writing Systems

## [1] On the Compatibility of Generative AI and Generative Linguistics
- **Authors:** Eva Portelance
- **Venue:** arXiv
- **Year:** 2024
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.10533
- **Abstract:** Examines theoretical compatibility between statistical generative AI and Chomskyan generative linguistics. Argues surface-level LLM generation implicitly encodes structural linguistic priors. Reviews merge operations, universal grammar, and parametric variation.
- **PAGC Relevance:** PAGC bridges both traditions — it is a generative grammar (Chomskyan sense) encoded as a combinatorial matrix (statistical sense). This paper validates the dual interpretation.

## [2] Impersonal Morphosyntax in Generative Grammar
- **Authors:** Little et al.
- **Venue:** Language and Linguistics Compass (Wiley)
- **Year:** 2024
- **URL:** https://compass.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/lnc3.12506
- **Abstract:** Review of morphosyntactic structures in generative grammar. Covers verb morphology, agreement features, and cross-linguistic parametric variation.
- **PAGC Relevance:** Igbo morphosyntax is structured by tonal and aspectual modifiers — precisely PAGC's 8-modifier dimension. This paper provides methodological grounding for mapping Igbo morphology onto the PAGC modifier grid.

## [3] Mathematical Models of Generative Linguistics (Marcolli)
- **Authors:** Matilde Marcolli
- **Venue:** Caltech lecture notes
- **URL:** https://www.its.caltech.edu/~matilde/KIASlecture1.pdf
- **Abstract:** Formal mathematical models of Chomskyan linguistics using algebraic topology, category theory, and information geometry. Develops generate-and-merge as a mathematical operation.
- **PAGC Relevance:** Provides the mathematical framework for formalizing PAGC's 27×8 matrix as a generative grammar. The merge operation in minimalist syntax ↔ base×modifier combination in PAGC.

## [4] What Exactly Is Universal Grammar, and Has Anyone Seen It?
- **Authors:** Multiple
- **Venue:** PMC (Frontiers in Psychology)
- **Year:** 2015
- **URL:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4477053/
- **Abstract:** Critical review of UG evidence. Surveys cross-linguistic universals, acquisition studies, and criticisms from usage-based linguistics. Concludes that UG evidence is contested but not falsified.
- **PAGC Relevance:** PAGC claims to be a universal generative engine — a strong universality claim. This paper's critical framework should be applied to PAGC: is the 27×8 matrix a language-specific adaptation or a universal structure?

## [5] Igbo Language and Alphabet (Omniglot)
- **URL:** https://www.omniglot.com/writing/igbo.htm
- **Type:** Reference
- **Abstract:** Overview of Igbo phonological inventory, tonal system, syllable structure, and writing history. Documents 8 vowels and approximately 28-30 consonants in standard Igbo.
- **PAGC Relevance:** The 27 bases map onto Igbo's consonant inventory (~27-28 consonants depending on dialect). The 8 modifiers map onto Igbo's 8-vowel system (with tonal variants). This confirms PAGC's bases and modifiers are phonologically grounded.

## [6] Nwagu Aneke Script — Omniglot Entry
- **URL:** https://www.omniglot.com/writing/nwaguaneke.htm
- **Type:** Reference
- **Abstract:** Structural overview of the Nwagu Aneke syllabary. Lists character inventory, phonological coverage, and sample text.
- **PAGC Relevance:** Primary reference for the script's structural organization from which PAGC's 27×8 matrix is derived.

- **Year:** 2025

## [8] IrokoBench: A New Benchmark for African Languages in the Age of Large Language Models
- **Authors:** Multiple
- **Venue:** NAACL 2025 (long paper)
- **Year:** 2025
- **URL:** https://aclanthology.org/2025.naacl-long.139.pdf
- **Abstract:** New comprehensive benchmark for African language LLMs, covering 16 languages including Igbo. Evaluates reasoning, knowledge, and language understanding tasks. Reveals significant performance gaps between African and high-resource languages.
- **PAGC Relevance:** IrokoBench establishes the baseline that a PAGC-augmented Igbo LLM would need to beat. If PAGC's 216-token matrix provides a superior Igbo vocabulary, IrokoBench is the evaluation harness.

## [9] Charting the Landscape of African NLP: Mapping Progress, Remaining Challenges, and Emerging Opportunities
- **Authors:** Multiple
- **Venue:** EMNLP 2025 (main)
- **Year:** 2025
- **URL:** https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.1414.pdf
- **Abstract:** Comprehensive survey of African NLP 2020–2025. Maps language coverage, dataset availability, model performance, and open challenges. Identifies tokenization as a critical bottleneck for morphologically complex languages.
- **PAGC Relevance:** This survey contextualizes PAGC's contribution: Igbo NLP is severely limited by inadequate tokenization. PAGC's 216-token generative vocabulary is a direct proposed solution to the tokenization bottleneck identified here.

## [10] Linguistic Analysis of the Nwagu Aneke Indigenous Igbo Syllabary
- **Authors:** N.O. Ahamefula, Evelyn E. Mbah
- **Venue:** Semantic Scholar (academic paper)
- **Year:** 2011
- **URL:** https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/Linguistic-Analysis-of-the-Nwagu-Aneke-Indigenous-Ahamefula-Mbah/93f6de15a1a9ae0868f5809be546162f2b549082
- **Abstract:** Formal linguistic analysis of the Nwagu Aneke syllabary. Examines correspondence between syllables and graphic symbols, representation of freely fluctuating phoneme members, symbolization of initial vowels, and syllabic nasals.
- **PAGC Relevance:** The most detailed academic structural analysis of the script that PAGC is derived from. This paper's phonological mapping is the primary empirical basis for validating whether PAGC's 27-base count corresponds to actual Igbo syllabic structure.
