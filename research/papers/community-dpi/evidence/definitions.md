# Definitional Vocabulary: Community-Led DPI (T7)

This file tracks the evolving vocabulary used to define "Community DPI" for an academic audience.

## Proposed Terms

### 1. Community-Layer Infrastructure (CLI)
- **Definition:** Digital systems (payments, records, coordination) owned and operated by non-state community institutions (town unions, cooperatives, informal associations).
- **Why it works:** Distinguishes from national-scale DPI while retaining the "infrastructure" utility frame.

### 2. Sub-State Digital Commons
- **Definition:** Shared digital resources managed under community governance rules (Ostrom-style) rather than market or state logic.
- **Why it works:** Appeals to Ostrom/Commons scholars.

### 3. Distributed Community Trust Anchors
- **Definition:** Localized technical or social mechanisms that provide verifiable truth for a specific community (e.g., a verifiable ledger of town union contributions).
- **Why it works:** Appeals to security/cryptography/trust researchers.

### 4. Hyper-Local DPI
- **Definition:** DPI components that are specialized for the unique governance needs and trust profiles of a single community.
- **Why it works:** Direct play on the "DPI" buzzword but emphasizes scale.

## Problematic Terms (Avoid)
- **"Informal DPI"** — Reviewers associate "informal" with "chaotic" or "unreliable".
- **"Shadow State Infrastructure"** — Sounds subversive or illegal.
- **"Private DPI"** — DPI is by definition public/collective; "private" implies market logic.
