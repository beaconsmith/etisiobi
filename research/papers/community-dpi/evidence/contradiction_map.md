# Contradiction Map

> Paper: `community-dpi`

| Tension | Supporting side | Opposing / caution side | Risk | Resolution strategy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| DPI concepts transfer to community scale | Interoperability/modularity are abstract infrastructure properties | DPI definitions are usually national, population-scale, and state-anchored | High | Frame as "community-layer infrastructure" unless equivalence is proven |
| Community institutions provide infrastructure | T11 town union evidence supports roads, schools, markets, coordination | Physical infrastructure provision does not prove digital infrastructure governance capacity | High | Use physical infrastructure as precedent, not proof |
| Polycentric DPI possibility | Polycentric governance supports multi-level institutional arrangements | DPI governance often depends on formal law, standards, and state capacity | Medium | Explore interoperability with national DPI rather than replacement |
