# Section 6: Conclusion — Building Durable Trust

Trust in informal institutions is a powerful but fragile resource. By moving from "recall-based trust" to "artifact-based trust," we provide community governance with the administrative infrastructure it needs to scale its impact.

## 6.1 Summary of the Model
The Artifact-First Trust model posits that digital artifacts are not just records, but are the primary mechanism for establishing procedural justice and institutional legitimacy. By adhering to the principles of receipts-over-recall, duty-to-document, and non-repudiable anchors, informal institutions can transition from "fragile orality" to "durable digital memory."

## 6.2 Final Takeaway
Digital governance for the Global South must solve the "Trust Deficit" at the community level. When a town union or village assembly can provide its members with verifiable "receipts" for every decision and disbursement, it creates a foundation for collective action that no amount of social recall can match. Artifact-First Trust is the first step toward a more transparent, accountable, and successful community-led future.
