# Section 4: Evidence — The Record-Keeping Reality in SE Nigeria

To understand the feasibility of the Artifact-First model, we examine the current record-keeping practices and failures in Southeast Nigerian town unions. This regional context provides a "lived reality" of the trust deficit created by a lack of verifiable artifacts.

## 4.1 The Fragility of Paper and Orality
While town unions often maintain "Minute Books," these paper records are inherently fragile. 
- **Loss and Damage:** In many rural communities, historical records are lost to dampness, fire, or poor storage, leading to a permanent loss of institutional memory.
- **Alteration and Contestation:** Leadership reports (CLEEN 2026) show that paper records are frequently contested, with members claiming that minutes were "doctored" or that pages were missing. This contestation is often the root cause of leadership crises.

## 4.2 The "Receipts" Demand in Community Finance
There is a high demand for verifiable evidence in community finance. ROSCAs and community "levies" are the lifeblood of rural development. However, without a transparent, shared ledger, members often feel that their contributions are not being properly tracked. The success of digital fintech in Nigeria suggests that community members are ready for "digital receipts" and value the non-repudiability they provide.

## 4.3 Pilot Observation: The Anchor Requirement
Early assessments of community coordination (Nwafor 2022) suggest that even when digital tools are introduced, they are only trusted if they provide a "permanent" record. Community members prioritize "anchoring"—the belief that once a record is made, it cannot be changed. This empirical observation directly supports our Principle 3 (Non-Repudiable Anchors).

## 4.4 Conclusion of Evidence
The evidence from Southeast Nigeria indicates that the problem is not a lack of *desire* for records, but a lack of *infrastructure* that can survive the pressures of informal governance. The Artifact-First model addresses this by providing a digital infrastructure that is as resilient as the social capital it serves.
