# Contradiction Map

> Paper: `artifact-first-trust`

| Tension | Supporting side | Opposing / caution side | Risk | Resolution strategy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Verifiable records improve procedural trust | Tyler, ISO 15489, SCITT provide theory/standards support | No direct community-level trust measurement yet | High | Say records align with known trust-generating conditions; do not claim measured trust effects |
| Formal records create accountability | ISO 15489, records management literature | Pozen and formalization critiques warn transparency can drift into surveillance or exclusion | High | Add privacy, consent, and minimal-disclosure constraints to design principles |
| Receipts as governance artifacts | SCITT receipt concept supports proof of registration | Receipts can prove registration without proving fairness or legitimacy | Medium | Treat receipts as necessary evidence artifacts, not sufficient legitimacy |
| Community memory vs. digital durability | Records-resilience sources support continuity | Oral witnessing may be socially legitimate and context-sensitive | Medium | Avoid portraying oral governance as defective; scope claim to digitally mediated/dispersed governance |
