# Source Gaps

> Paper: `artifact-first-trust`

## Critical

- **Africa/Nigeria community recordkeeping:** Need sources on how community associations, informal finance groups, customary institutions, or local organizations preserve decisions and records.
- **Regional grounding:** Eghweree 2021 is useful but insufficient. Add at least 3 local/regional anchors before promotion.
- **Formalization critique:** Need sources on how documentation requirements can exclude low-literacy participants, increase surveillance, or shift power to record-holders.

## Important

- **Design theory method:** Add Gregor & Hevner / Walls-style design theory sources to justify deriving design principles from theory and standards.
- **Empirical trust evidence:** No direct evidence yet that artifact-first records increase trust in community governance. Phrase claims as theoretical alignment, not measured effect.
