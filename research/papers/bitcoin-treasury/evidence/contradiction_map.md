# Contradiction Map

> Paper: `bitcoin-treasury`

| Tension | Supporting side | Opposing / caution side | Risk | Resolution strategy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Blockchain improves treasury transparency | Bitcoin/ledger sources and World Bank digital finance sources support tamper-evidence | Ferreira 2026 and DAO failures show decentralization does not solve governance | High | Lead with failure analysis; position blockchain as one design substrate, not a cure |
| Distributed ledger vs. centralized database | Distributed systems offer tamper-resistance and independent verification | Centralized tamper-evident databases may be cheaper and easier | High | Specify conditions where community sovereignty or hostile turnover justifies distributed settlement |
| Public auditability vs. financial privacy | Treasury transparency supports accountability | Public ledgers can expose sensitive financial behavior | High | Require selective disclosure/privacy-preserving audit principles |
| Community finance vs. crypto speculation | Community treasury framing is governance-oriented | "Bitcoin" title may trigger investment/speculation reading | Medium | Consider title softening to "verifiable settlement infrastructure" unless Bitcoin-specific argument is essential |
