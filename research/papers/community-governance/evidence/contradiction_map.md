# Contradiction Map

> Paper: `community-governance`

| Tension | Supporting side | Opposing / caution side | Risk | Resolution strategy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Community institutions as governance engines vs. elite-capture risk | T11-E, T11-F, T11-U show development and coordination functions | T11-S, T11-V and broader neopatrimonial/governance critique warn of weak accountability | High | State that community institutions are governance actors, not automatically good governance actors |
| Polycentric governance transfer to SE Nigeria | Ostrom 1990, Hess & Ostrom 2003, Aguerre 2024 support polycentric framing | Ostrom did not study Nigerian digital governance directly | Medium | Use Ostrom as analytical vocabulary, not proof of effectiveness |
| Infrastructure delivery vs. digital transformation | Town union sources support roads, schools, markets, COVID coordination | Digital-specific town-union evidence is sparse | High | Frame digital transformation as an extension opportunity and measurement gap |
| Gender-inclusive governance vs. male-dominated town union narratives | Chuku and Igbo dual-sex scholarship support women’s authority | Town union literature often centers male leadership | Medium | Include women’s associations and gendered authority as part of community institution taxonomy |
