# Regional Context

> Paper: `community-governance`

## Primary Context

Southeast Nigeria, with emphasis on Igbo-speaking states and community institutions such as town unions, age grades, women's associations, village assemblies, ROSCAs/esusu groups, and diaspora hometown associations.

## Strong Regional Anchors

| Source | Use |
|---|---|
| T11-E | Empirical support that town unions contribute to rural/community development in Southeast Nigeria |
| T11-F | Frames town unions as fourth-tier governance actors, including COVID-era coordination |
| T11-U | Adds selected-community evidence from Imo State |
| T11-V | Critical assessment of town unions as local-government alternatives |
| Chuku 2015/2022 | Balances male-dominated town-union story with Igbo women's political authority |
| UChicago 2024 working paper | Supports deliberative/gender-complementarity framing |
| IDiaspora/UNDP 2012 | Supports translocal/diaspora association context |

## Regional Caveats

- The current evidence is stronger for infrastructure and development coordination than for explicitly digital governance.
- Critical/failure evidence from Southeast Nigeria is still thin.
- Town unions are overrepresented; age grades, women's groups, and ROSCAs need more coverage.
