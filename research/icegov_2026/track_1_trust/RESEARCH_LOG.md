Source T1-C
Citation: ISO 15489-1:2016. Information and Documentation — Records Management — Part 1: Concepts and Principles.
Year: 2016
Type: International standard
Region: Global
Cluster: Records management / archival integrity / evidence
Relevance: Defines authenticity, reliability, integrity, usability of records
Confidence: 5/5
Usability: direct
Key claims: (1) Authentic record: provenance guaranteed. (2) Reliable: full and accurate representation; created at time of event by those with direct knowledge. (5) Linkages between records documenting related transactions must be maintained.
Notes: Essential for defining what makes a record system trustworthy.
