# A+ Lab Standard

> Etisiobi must operate as a research lab, not a document generator.

## Diagnosis

The lab's current failure mode is not a missing PDF, validator, or benchmark.
The failure mode is upstream: too many artifacts are allowed to look like
papers before they have earned paper status.

An A+ lab does not manufacture quality by patching objections one at a time.
It prevents weak outputs from entering the publication lane.

## Non-Negotiable Standard

Every research branch must pass through these gates:

1. `RESEARCH_PROGRAM` — topic exists, artifact/source base identified.
2. `SOURCE_DOSSIER` — primary sources, rights status, and source uncertainty recorded.
3. `CLAIM_MODEL` — core claims, contradictions, and falsification conditions exist.
4. `EXPERIMENTAL_RESULT` — an audit, experiment, theorem, dataset, or negative result exists.
5. `PAPER_CANDIDATE` — prior art, result, method, and venue fit are strong enough to justify drafting.
6. `SUBMISSION_CANDIDATE` — full manuscript, complete citations, figures/tables, and review trace exist.
7. `PUBLIC_RELEASE_READY` — authority, rights, source review, and human approval pass.

No branch may skip stages. A compiled PDF is not a stage.

## Portfolio Discipline

The Nwagu Aneke program may maintain many research branches, but the lab must
not treat ten branches as ten publishable papers at once.

Default operating rule:

- Maintain many research branches.
- Promote at most two branches into article hardening at any time.
- Park branches that lack article-specific novelty, source review, or a result.
- Kill branches that cannot produce an external contribution.
- Render working PDFs only as internal review artifacts, never as submission signals.

## What Counts As A Result

A result must be one of:

- source-critical reconstruction that changes what can be claimed;
- reproducible audit that corrects or falsifies a prior claim;
- empirical experiment with baselines and measurable outcome;
- formal proof or impossibility result;
- reusable dataset, benchmark, or protocol with validation;
- negative result that changes the research direction.

Formatting, citations, standards mapping, and traceability are supporting
infrastructure. They are not the discovery.

## Promotion Rules

A branch cannot become `PAPER_CANDIDATE` unless it has:

- a one-sentence result that matters outside the repo;
- article-specific prior art, not generic lab standards;
- a source/rights status;
- a method that a reviewer can reproduce or inspect;
- a contradiction or falsification path;
- a reviewer attack surface;
- a decision trace showing why this branch outranks alternatives.

A branch cannot become `SUBMISSION_CANDIDATE` unless it has:

- full manuscript depth, not a short formatted note;
- complete bibliography with verified sources;
- figures/tables backed by files;
- passed claim gate;
- passed review-team trace;
- passed rights/authority review;
- passed compile/package audit.

## Lab-Level Metric

The headline metric is not number of PDFs. It is:

```text
research_quality = external_result_strength
                 + evidence_strength
                 + novelty_after_prior_art
                 + reproducibility
                 + reviewer_survivability
                 - overclaim_risk
                 - authority_rights_risk
                 - weak_branch_sprawl
```

If a revision increases prose polish but does not improve this metric, reject it.
