# Submission Rules

> ICEGOV 2026 submission requirements and pre-submission checklist.

---

## Venue Details

- **Conference:** 18th International Conference on Theory and Practice of Electronic Governance (ICEGOV 2026)
- **Deadline:** May 8, 2026
- **Format:** ACM two-column format (`acmart` document class, `sigconf` style)
- **Submission system:** EDAS
- **Review type:** Double-blind

---

## Paper Types and Page Limits

| Type | Pages (ACM format) | What reviewers expect |
|------|--------------------|-----------------------|
| Research Paper | 8–10 pages | Full contribution, methodology, evaluation, results |
| Ongoing Research | 4–6 pages | Clear problem, methodology described, partial or planned evaluation |
| Short Paper | 2–4 pages | Focused contribution, concise argument |

Page limits include references.

---

## Double-Blind Requirements

- [ ] No author names in the document
- [ ] No institutional affiliations
- [ ] No self-identifying project names (use neutral placeholders)
- [ ] No URLs that reveal authorship (e.g., GitHub repos, personal sites)
- [ ] No "our previous work" phrasing that identifies authors
- [ ] Self-citations in third person: "Smith et al. [5] found…" not "we previously showed [5]…"
- [ ] No acknowledgments section in submitted version
- [ ] Location cues neutralized where they would identify a specific team

---

## ACM Formatting Checklist

- [ ] Uses `\documentclass[sigconf]{acmart}`
- [ ] CCS concepts included
- [ ] Keywords included
- [ ] Abstract ≤ 250 words
- [ ] References formatted correctly (BibTeX)
- [ ] No orphan headings (heading at bottom of column with no following text)
- [ ] Figures and tables have captions
- [ ] All figures are readable in black and white
- [ ] PDF compiles without errors

---

## Pre-Submission Audit

- [ ] Track fit verified — paper matches track description
- [ ] Page limit met
- [ ] All citations compile and are real
- [ ] No broken cross-references
- [ ] Abstract honestly matches paper content
- [ ] Contribution claim is in the introduction
- [ ] Related work is fair to existing literature
- [ ] Methodology section is honest about what was actually done
- [ ] Limitations section exists and is genuine
- [ ] All claims in the paper are supported by evidence in `claim_map.md`
- [ ] Blinding check passes (see `blinding_check.md`)
- [ ] PDF opens correctly in standard readers
- [ ] EDAS metadata matches paper content
