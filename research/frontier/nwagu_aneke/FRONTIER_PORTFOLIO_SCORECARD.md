# Frontier Portfolio Scorecard

## Purpose

This scorecard prevents the open frontier program from collapsing into either
the easiest paper or the loudest speculation. It ranks branches by information
gain, evidence integrity, and ability to unlock other lanes.

Scores are provisional. They guide sprint selection; they do not promote claims.

## Scoring Dimensions

Each branch is scored 0 to 5:

- `evidence_maturity`
- `novelty`
- `falsifiability`
- `reproducibility`
- `rights_readiness`
- `technical_feasibility`
- `public_value`
- `strategic_leverage`
- `time_to_proof`
- `unlock_power`

Penalty flags:

- `overclaim_risk`
- `rights_blocker`
- `source_blocker`
- `tool_capture_risk`
- `easy_paper_bias`
