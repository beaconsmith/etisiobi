**Subject:** Research inquiry: Nwagu Aneke Research Project proposal — PL8021.N5 N93 1983 quarto

Dear Librarian,

I am conducting source-critical research into the Nwagụ Aneke Igbo syllabary, particularly its sign inventory and early documentation.

Your catalogue lists *The Nwagu Aneke Research Project proposal*, University of Nigeria, approximately 1983–1989, 11 illustrated pages, under **PL8021.N5 N93 1983 quarto** (African Art collection; barcode **39088008864084**; OCLC **40642457**). The record also mentions a two-page letter by Chukwuma Azuonye inserted in this copy.

Could you confirm whether the copy is currently available for research consultation, and whether pages **5** (syllabary chart) and **9** (writing sample) are present and legible? If they are, please advise on any permissible research-access or limited reproduction options, applicable fees, and rights or attribution requirements.

I am not requesting public release, unrestricted reproduction, or access to private materials. If a different department handles this holding, a referral would be appreciated.

Thank you for your assistance.

The Beaconsmith Collective Research Team

## Why this is a *narrow* request

Azuonye (1992) identifies the project proposal p. 5 as an underlying source for the chart; Ahamefula (2010) reproduces a text sample with Roman-script Umuleri transcription and English translation, attributing it to proposal p. 9. The Smithsonian holding may allow the lab to verify those source references and provenance.

**Do not** claim the 1991 alphabetical list prepared by Donatus Nwoga is inside this earlier proposal; the catalogue does not establish that.

**Send gate:** owner reviews wording, confirms correct current contact and rights posture, authorizes external communication, and records any reply as a dated source note.
