\begin{letter}{Editor-in-Chief \\ Government Information Quarterly}

\opening{Dear Editor,}

I am pleased to submit the manuscript titled \textbf{"The Community Digital Governance Index (CDGI): Measuring Community-Layer Digital Governance in Southeast Nigeria"} for consideration for publication in \textit{Government Information Quarterly}.

Current digital governance indices (such as EGDI and GTMI) are predominantly state-centered, focusing on municipal and national service maturity. However, in regions like Southeast Nigeria, critical governance functions—including development finance, credit allocation, and dispute resolution—are performed by community institutions like town unions and diaspora savings groups. These institutions operate in a "measurement gap" where their digital maturity and record accountability remain unobserved by traditional benchmarks.

This paper introduces the CDGI, a seven-dimension framework designed specifically to measure the quality of governance records, community control, and process accountability at the community layer. We demonstrate the framework’s utility through a design-science approach, showing how verifiable records can surface concrete institutional weaknesses that state-centered measures routinely miss.

Given GIQ's focus on the intersection of information technology and government policy, I believe this work on "below-the-state" governance measurement will be of significant interest to your readership, particularly those interested in ICT4D, local governance, and digital accountability.

This manuscript is original work and has not been published elsewhere, nor is it currently under consideration by another journal.

Thank you for your time and consideration.
