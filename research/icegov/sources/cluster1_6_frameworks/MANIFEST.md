## Key Gaps Identified for This Cluster

1. EGDI/GTMI/DGI all measure national-level, top-down state capacity — none operationalize community-layer governance
2. All major indices use perceptions-based or service-availability proxies, not verifiable artifact-based indicators
3. Africa consistently scores below global average; no index captures *why* at community level
4. Existing composite indicator methodology (OECD/JRC) applies to national data — needs adaptation for community-generated data

## Critical Quotes Saved

- EGDI = "equally weighted composite of OSI, TII, and HCI"
- Africa EGDI = 0.4247 (significantly below global average)
- OECD DGI dimensions: Digital by design, Data-driven public sector, Government as platform, Open by default, User-driven, Proactiveness
- GTMI covers: core government systems, online service delivery, digital citizen engagement, GovTech enablers
- Heeks: "ranking obsession" creates measurement bias
- WJP: Informal Justice acknowledged but excluded from aggregated score
