## Key Arguments Enabled

1. **Oroma IS a DPI stack**: Ichi = digital identity layer; Akpa Oroma = community payments layer; Truth Layer = verifiable data exchange. Maps exactly to World Bank/UNDP DPI definition.

2. **CARE Principles apply to community records**: Community-led governance records must respect Collective Benefit, Authority to Control, Responsibility, Ethics — not just technical verifiability.

3. **WhatsApp governance is the broken baseline**: Existing literature (AJIC 2024, West Africa political communication studies) documents how informal messaging apps fail as governance infrastructure.

4. **ICegov 2025 left the community-layer gap open**: Nigeria had 16 papers at ICEGOV 2025 but none addressed community-layer governance records or indicator frameworks for non-state institutions.
