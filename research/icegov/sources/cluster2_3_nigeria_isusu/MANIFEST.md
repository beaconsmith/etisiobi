## Key Facts Saved

- **17%** of Nigerian adults save in informal savings group (World Bank GHSP data)
- **23 million** Nigerians benefited from CSDP; 16,000+ communities
- Only **4.9%** of Nigerian households received government COVID assistance (March-July 2020)
- Town unions successfully executed: roads, schools, markets, water, electricity — all self-funded
- Esusu: operates on "oath of allegiance and mutual trust" — no formal legal mechanism
- e-ROSCA DRC experiment: ~90% contribution rate; 62% full four-day compliance
- ROSCA membership: up to 95% in some African nations (Congo, Cameroon, Gambia, Ivory Coast)
- Town union leaders "directly accountable to communities" through consensus selection
- Key failure mode: WhatsApp screenshots + social trust alone — breaks predictably

## Critical Argument Enabled

Town unions in Southeast Nigeria are **already a fourth-tier governance institution** — they build infrastructure, manage community funds, resolve disputes, and maintain social order. But their records are fragile (WhatsApp screenshots, not cryptographic proofs). Oroma formalizes their existing governance loop with verifiable records. The OGI Framework measures whether this formalization actually improves governance quality.
