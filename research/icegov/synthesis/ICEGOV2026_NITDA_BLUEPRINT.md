## Core Contribution in Track 6 Language

**The Oroma Governance Indicator (OGI) Framework** — a measurement instrument that operationalises:

1. **Record Verifiability** — Truth Layer completeness rate; ISO 15489 authenticity properties
2. **Decision Participation Rate** — proposals raised vs. quorum achieved; governance legitimacy
3. **Treasury Transparency Index** — on-chain audit coverage; solvency provability without privacy loss
4. **Dispute Resolution Legitimacy** — dispute rate + resolution speed + outcome contestation rate
5. **Credential Portability Score** — Ichi module adoption; verifiable community identity
6. **Community Autonomy Score** — state-dependency ratio; polycentricity measurement
7. **Financial Inclusion Depth** — first-time formal financial participants; ROSCA digitisation baseline

Each indicator maps to:
- An observable artifact or on-chain event (not a perception survey)
- A governance theory construct (Ostrom, CARE, ISO 15489)
- A mainstream benchmark gap (what EGDI/DGI/GTMI miss)
