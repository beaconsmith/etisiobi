## The Mathematical Core

### Exceptional Properties of 27
- **27 lines on a cubic surface:** Every smooth cubic surface in projective 3-space contains exactly 27 lines. The symmetry group of these lines is the Weyl group W(E₆), of order 51,840.
- **Albert algebra (exceptional Jordan algebra):** The unique 27-dimensional formally real Jordan algebra that is not special. Its automorphism group is F₄; its structure group is E₆.
- **E₆ fundamental representation:** 27-dimensional. Basis given by the 27 lines on a cubic surface.

### Exceptional Properties of 8
- **Octonions:** 8-dimensional normed division algebra. Only normed division algebras are R, C, H (quaternions), O (octonions) — dimensions 1, 2, 4, 8.
- **SU(3) generators:** 8 Gell-Mann matrices generate SU(3), the symmetry group of the strong force.
- **E₈ root system:** 240 roots; divided by 30 = 8. E₈ is the largest exceptional Lie group.

- 6 = 2 × 3 (product of first two primes)
