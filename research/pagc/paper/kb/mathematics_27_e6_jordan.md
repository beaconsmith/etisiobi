## [1] The 27 Lines on a Cubic Surface (Cayley–Salmon Theorem)
- **Source:** Classical algebraic geometry (Cayley 1849, Salmon 1849)
- **URL:** https://math.ucr.edu/home/baez/visual_insight/20160215%20-%2027%20Lines%20on%20a%20Cubic%20Surface.html
- **Statement:** Every smooth cubic surface over an algebraically closed field contains exactly 27 lines.
- **Symmetry group:** The symmetry group of the 27 lines' incidence structure is isomorphic to the Weyl group W(E₆), of order 51,840.
- **PAGC Relevance:** PAGC's 27 bases possess the same combinatorial richness as the 27 lines — each base relates to every other via the 8-modifier network, producing an incidence structure with E₆-level symmetry potential.

## [2] The Exceptional Jordan Algebra (Albert Algebra)
- **Source:** Albert (1934); extensive literature
- **URL:** https://cp4space.hatsya.com/2020/10/28/the-exceptional-jordan-algebra/
- **Dimension:** 27 (real vector space of 3×3 self-adjoint octonionic matrices)
- **Key property:** E₆ is the group of determinant-preserving linear transformations of this algebra. It is the only finite-dimensional formally real Jordan algebra that is not a special Jordan algebra.
- **URL (Moufang loop connection):** https://www.sciencedirect.com/article/pii/002186939090176O
- **PAGC Relevance:** The Albert algebra's 27-dimensionality means that PAGC's 27 bases span an exceptional algebraic space — one with the same dimensionality as the only exceptional Jordan algebra. If PAGC's bases satisfy certain algebraic properties, the full system may exhibit E₆ symmetry.
