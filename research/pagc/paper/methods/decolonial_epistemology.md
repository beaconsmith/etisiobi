# Methods: Decolonial Epistemology & African Knowledge Systems

## [1] Decolonising Science, Technology, Engineering and Mathematics (STEM) in an Age of Technocolonialism
- **Authors:** Nhemachena, Hlabangane, Matowanyika
- **Venue:** Book (Langaa RPCIG)
- **Year:** 2019
- **ISBN:** 9789956551866
- **URL:** https://www.jstor.org/stable/j.ctv10h9fqz
- **Abstract:** Recentres African Indigenous Knowledge and Belief Systems in STEM education and practice. Critiques technocolonialism — the use of Western STEM frameworks to subordinate non-Western knowledge production. Proposes pluriversality as epistemological framework.
- **PAGC Relevance:** PAGC is an act of epistemic decolonization — a non-Western generative system claiming universality from an Indigenous starting point. This book provides the epistemological framework for PAGC's claim to stand alongside (not beneath) Western information theory.

## [2] Decolonising Mathematics Education: Reimagining Pedagogical Practices Through Indigenous Knowledge Systems in Zambia
- **Authors:** Multiple
- **Venue:** African Journal of Research in Mathematics, Science and Technology Education (Taylor & Francis)
- **Year:** 2026
- **URL:** https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/18117295.2026.2633890
- **Abstract:** Study examining integration of Indigenous Knowledge Systems into mathematics education in Zambia. Documents culturally responsive mathematics pedagogy grounded in local knowledge structures.
- **PAGC Relevance:** PAGC's mathematical structure (27×8 matrix) can serve as the basis for Indigenous mathematics curriculum — a combinatorial system whose properties students discover through their own language rather than through imported abstractions.

## [3] African Epistemologies and the Decolonial Curriculum
- **Authors:** Multiple
- **Venue:** Acta Academica
- **URL:** https://journals.ufs.ac.za/index.php/aa/article/view/5762
- **Abstract:** Proposes African epistemological frameworks for curriculum decolonization. Distinguishes Ubuntu (relational knowing), oral transmission, and communal knowledge validation from Western individualist epistemology.
- **PAGC Relevance:** PAGC's lexical cache encodes moral/equity terms — a computational instantiation of Ubuntu epistemology. The cache represents communally validated knowledge crystallized into a high-frequency prior.

## [4] The Roles of Epistemology and Decoloniality in Addressing Power Dynamics in University Education
- **Authors:** Multiple
- **Venue:** Taylor & Francis
- **Year:** 2024
- **URL:** https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/14767724.2024.2335661
- **Abstract:** Examines how epistemological choices in university education reproduce or challenge colonial power structures. Argues that knowledge system pluralism is a prerequisite for epistemic justice.
- **PAGC Relevance:** PAGC's universality claim must pass the epistemic justice test: does it expand the space of valid knowledge systems or merely reframe an African system in Western informational terms? This paper provides the evaluative framework.

## [5] Navigating the Decolonial Frontier: Leadership Perspectives on Epistemic Justice in South African Higher Education
- **Authors:** Multiple
- **Venue:** ScienceDirect
- **Year:** 2025
- **URL:** https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0883035525002897
- **Abstract:** Empirical study of decolonization implementation in South African universities. Documents successes and resistances in shifting from Western epistemological dominance.
- **PAGC Relevance:** Documents the institutional landscape PAGC must navigate to gain academic legitimacy. The paper's findings on resistance points predict where PAGC's universality claim will face the greatest friction.

## [6] Africanising Indigeneity: Decolonising Identities, Knowledges, and Resilience in the Anthropocene
- **Authors:** Multiple
- **Venue:** Taylor & Francis (African Identities)
- **Year:** 2026
- **URL:** https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/00020184.2026.2643608
- **Abstract:** Frames African Indigenous knowledge systems as resilient, adaptive responses to environmental and colonial pressures. Argues for their active role in Anthropocene problem-solving.
- **PAGC Relevance:** PAGC's design — redundancy for robustness, equity terms in the lexical cache — encodes exactly the adaptive resilience described here. PAGC is a computational formalization of Indigenous knowledge resilience principles.
