# Etisiobi
*Research rooted in evidence, from Southeast Nigeria.*

Etisiobi is The Beaconsmith Collective's research lab. We investigate African knowledge systems, community governance, and the design of trustworthy technology. We preserve sources, test interpretations, and show what the evidence supports, what remains uncertain, and what has been ruled out.

The name **Etisiobi** means *the tree from my heart* in Igbo. The lab honours the late Prof. Etisiobi Mark Ndiokwelu, educator and scholar, and reflects a simple commitment: ambitious ideas must have roots that others can inspect.

**How to read a finding:** a source observation, an interpretation, and an experimental result are different things. Our [claim maturity model](CLAIM_MATURITY_MODEL.md) and [evidence policy](EVIDENCE_POLICY.md) explain the distinction. A polished paper draft or demonstration is not proof that a claim has passed review.

## Current status and limitations

The repository contains working research, source dossiers, experiments and paper drafts at different stages of maturity. See the [cross-program atlas](wiki/index.md) for navigation and the relevant programme files for current state. Some historical documents contain old dates or readiness language; follow the dated evidence and review gates, not a filename or formatted PDF. Rights and community authority can limit public release even where source material is technically accessible.

Etisiobi is the research twin of Oroma, a Beaconsmith product. Research and product decisions are connected through review, not automatic promotion.
