## Background

Current evidence summary:

- Nwagu Aneke is an Igbo writing system discussed in Azuonye's account and
  public script-status resources.
- The current repo foundation separates a 26 by 8 source-observed table
  from a derived 27/216 f/v expansion.
- More source review is needed before this becomes a proposed character
  repertoire.

Required before submission:

- Stable history of the script.
- Time period and location of use.
- User community or scholarly/preservation community definition.
- Evidence of usage independent of unsupported claims.
- Relationship to other West African and Igbo-adjacent scripts.

Current state:

`PARTIAL`
