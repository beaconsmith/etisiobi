## Ndebe / Ńdébé

Current decision: `SEPARATE_CREATOR_AUTHORITY_REQUIRED`

Ndebe is a separate modern Igbo script project. Its public site describes it as
a modern writing system for Igbo, invented by Lotanna Igwe-Odunze in 2009 and
refined over a decade. The site describes Ndebe as a syllabary with stems,
radicals, vowel markers, numbers, symbols, punctuation, and a large complete
syllabary. It also states that people should not represent or submit Ndebe to
government, company, body, or organization without contacting the creator, who
will represent or submit it.

Source URL:

- https://ndebe.org/

Working interpretation:

Do not bundle Ndebe into a Nwagu Aneke proposal. Do not submit or represent
Ndebe unless the creator or authorized representative explicitly participates
and resolves IP/CLA/endorsement questions. Ndebe may be used only as a
comparative case in research writing, with clear separation from Nwagu Aneke.

## Proposal Strategy

Nwagu Aneke, Ndebe, Nsibidi/New Nsibidi, and any future Igbo-adjacent script
lead must remain separate proposal objects unless standards experts recommend a
combined strategy. Bundling them prematurely would create authority, repertoire,
and IP confusion.
