# Independent Submitter Pathway for Nwagu Aneke

Status: `INDEPENDENT_SUBMITTER_PATH_OPEN_PACKAGE_INCOMPLETE`

An independent researcher can prepare and submit a Unicode new-script proposal
if the proposal satisfies SEWG requirements and the submitter/authors complete
the required Unicode CLA process. The current blocker is not permission to think
or draft. The blocker is that the evidence package is incomplete.

## What The Independent Submitter Can Do Now

- Draft a preliminary standards inquiry.
- Draft a proposal skeleton using the new-script template.
- Compile source bibliography and usage evidence.
- Build a proposed repertoire table, clearly marked as provisional.
- Identify candidate character names and property questions.
- Identify what source images or examples can be cited, reproduced, or only
  described.
- Contact SEI or relevant experts for preliminary review.

## What The Independent Submitter Cannot Honestly Do Yet

- Claim Nwagu Aneke is Unicode-proposal-ready.
- Submit the current readiness article as a complete proposal.
- Treat 27/216 as source-observed.
- Publish source images, glyph crops, or manuscript examples without rights
  clearance or citable public sources.
- Claim creator/community endorsement that has not been obtained.
- Bypass CLA, IP-holder, font/licence, or proposal-form requirements.

## Minimum Evidence Package To Remove The Submission Block

1. A source-reviewed repertoire table.
2. Character-glyph distinction for every proposed unit.
3. Representative glyph evidence with source captions and rights status.
4. Usage, stability, and plain-text interchange evidence.
5. Directionality, ordering, punctuation, numbers, segmentation, line breaking,
   shaping, and normalization notes.
6. Candidate character names and Unicode property recommendations.
7. Font or font plan with licence.
8. Submitter and author CLA plan.
9. Potential IP-holder assessment.
10. Draft proposal PDF and ISO/IEC 10646 summary information.
