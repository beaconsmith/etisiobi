# NWR-009 Authority Role Map

Seed: `NWR-009`

Source question: Which review roles are needed for source rights and community benefit

Evidence layer: authority_rights

Claim ceiling: artifact_not_paper_candidate

## Bounded Artifact

This role map separates review functions so authority work does not collapse
into a single vague approval step.

| Role | Review focus | Evidence needed | Non-approval states |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rights authority reviewer | Source use and reproduction limits | Rights basis and exclusion list | defer, reject, needs narrower scope |
| Community authority reviewer | Benefit, harm, representation | Benefit-risk ledger and public framing | defer, reject, request reframing |
| Source dossier reviewer | Source trace and uncertainty | Witness inventory and contradiction map | defer, reject, needs source access |
| Preservation reviewer | Metadata package boundary | RO-Crate and candidate files | defer, reject, remove files |
| Data protection reviewer | Private or sensitive data risk | Candidate and excluded path audit | defer, reject, redact |

## Evidence Layer

The artifact is authority_rights infrastructure. It records roles, not people.

## Falsification Gate

The map fails if a branch can reach public release without at least one role
able to reject or defer it.

## Rights And Authority Guardrail

No reviewer identity or private contact data belongs in this artifact.

## What This Teaches Oroma

Governance is stronger when refusal and deferral are first-class states, not
awkward exceptions.
