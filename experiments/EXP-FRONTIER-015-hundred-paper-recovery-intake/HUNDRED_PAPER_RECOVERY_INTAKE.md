# 100-Paper Recovery Intake

This packet turns the backlog pressure into a governed research intake. It
creates 100 research seeds, not manuscripts, findings, article claims, or
publication packages.

The operating rule is seed-only: every row names a question, evidence layer,
first artifact, falsification gate, rights or authority gate, lab payoff, and
next action. The packet is a queue for disciplined work. It is not a paper
factory and does not assert that any seed is mature.

Zero manuscript outputs were created. No dependency installation, private data
download, external submission, public release claim, rights clearance, authority
approval, model run, or archive action occurred.

## What This Teaches Oroma

Oroma should inherit the same discipline: many branches can be explored quickly
when every branch carries its evidence layer, failure condition, authority
constraint, and next proof. Speed comes from routing uncertainty cleanly, not
from hiding it.

## Packet Contents

- `manifest.json` records the seed-only decision and blocked promotions.
- `research_seeds.csv` contains exactly 100 seed records.
- `research_seed_schema.json` defines the allowed seed fields.
- `claim_gate_matrix.csv` keeps each evidence layer below promotion.
- `sprint_selection.json` selects 12 seed-only records for the next triage pass.

## Exact Next Action

Select the first 12 seed-only records for source and lab triage, then create one
artifact per selected seed without producing manuscripts or readiness claims.
