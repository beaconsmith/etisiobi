# EXP-FRONTIER-006 Analysis

The atlas records 54 transfer mechanisms across OCR, epigraphy, program
induction, cultural transmission, African NLP, Indigenous data governance,
cultural-heritage knowledge graphs, font/input systems, annotation tooling, data
versioning, and AI evaluation.

The important methodological change is that every external source must supply:

- a mechanism,
- a Nwagụ research object,
- required evidence,
- an experiment,
- a negative control,
- a claim ceiling,
- a phase gate,
- a rights risk.

This prevents the earlier failure mode: collecting impressive links and then
turning them into loose analogies.

The atlas remains not prior-art verified. Its next job is to drive ranked
experiments, especially:

1. Digital Twin / evidence graph.
2. Latent Glyph Grammar Challenge.
3. Script Digital Survivability Matrix.
4. AI Epistemic Integrity Benchmark.
5. Full-Word Sign Allocation Study.
