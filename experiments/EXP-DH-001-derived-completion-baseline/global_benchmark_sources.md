## Local Interpretation

DH-001 is benchmarked against general research-quality properties:

- runnable artifact;
- documented data and metrics;
- baseline comparisons;
- adversarial/source-safety cases;
- transparent limitations;
- no source/derived layer promotion;
- evidence of value beyond labels.

The last criterion is the breakthrough criterion. DH-001 currently fails it.
