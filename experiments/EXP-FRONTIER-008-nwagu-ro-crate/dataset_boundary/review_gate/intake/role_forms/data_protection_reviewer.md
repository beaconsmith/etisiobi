# Data Protection Reviewer Form

Role ID: `data_protection_reviewer`

Agent may not approve this review.

## Review Packet

Inspect the dataset-boundary checklist, candidate files, excluded files,
reviewer roles, validator controls, review gate, and review requests.

## Focus

Assess privacy, human-response handling, private-data exclusion, and any risk
of identifiable material entering the candidate metadata package.

## Required Output

Return `PASS`, `REJECT`, or `REVISE`.

Include summary, files reviewed, blocking issues, and attestation that this is
real human review.

## Claim Ceiling

`review_intake_ready_not_human_review`
