# Global Benchmark Norms Used

This internal gate borrows conservative benchmark expectations from public
research norms. It does not claim that DH-003 has met a public benchmark,
leaderboard, publication, or global novelty standard.

## References

- ACM Artifact Review and Badging:
  <https://www.acm.org/publications/policies/artifact-review-badging>
- NeurIPS Paper Checklist:
  <https://neurips.cc/public/guides/PaperChecklist>
- NeurIPS Datasets and Benchmarks guidance:
  <https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2023/CallForDatasetsBenchmarks>
- Stanford CRFM HELM:
  <https://crfm.stanford.edu/helm/>

## Adapted Local Criteria

- reproducible local runner and test;
- named datasets and source artifacts;
- baseline comparison with a visible weak baseline;
- adversarial and false-positive coverage;
- transparent metrics;
- explicit claim ceiling;
- stage discipline before paper or release promotion.
