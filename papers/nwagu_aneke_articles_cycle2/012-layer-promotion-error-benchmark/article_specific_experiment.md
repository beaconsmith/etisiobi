# Article-Specific Experiment

Experiment label: Layer-promotion benchmark experiment.

Research question: can layer-promotion error be scored as an explicit benchmark
task for artifact-first autonomous research?

Hypothesis: a layer-labeled gate will reject unsupported promotions from
derived, speculative, or blocked claims into source-observed claims while
preserving safe source and derived statements.

Materials:

- `experiments/EXP-NA-012/results.json`
- `experiments/EXP-NA-012/data/layer_promotion_cases.csv`
- `experiments/EXP-NA-012/analysis.md`
- `experiments/EXP-NA-012/decision.md`

Result: the benchmark contains twelve claim cases. The gate rejected all six
unsafe promotion cases and preserved all six safe statements. The result is a
bounded internal methods result, not a public release or venue-acceptance claim.
