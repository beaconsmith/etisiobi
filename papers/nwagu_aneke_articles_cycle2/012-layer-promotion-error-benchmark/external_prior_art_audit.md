# External Prior-Art Audit

Article 012 is positioned against four prior-art clusters.

1. STORM and Co-STORM show that perspective-guided question asking can improve
   long-form knowledge curation. Article 012 does not reproduce their systems;
   it adapts the perspective discipline to source/derived claim safety.
2. Scientific claim verification and citation-grounded generation show why
   support relations must be evaluated rather than assumed.
3. TEI, IIIF, Web Annotation, PROV-O, RO-Crate, DataCite, CIDOC CRM, FAIR, and
   CARE provide infrastructure for representation, provenance, packaging, and
   governance.
4. Nwagu Aneke source/status references provide the artifact boundary and keep
   the source-observed layer at 26 by 8, with 27/216 marked as derived.

The article-specific contribution is the benchmark framing: layer-promotion
error is treated as a measurable task over source-observed, derived,
speculative, and blocked claim cases. This does not claim novelty over STORM,
claim verification, or cultural-heritage standards.
