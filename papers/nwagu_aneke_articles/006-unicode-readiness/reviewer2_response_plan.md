# Reviewer 2 Response Plan

Article: `ARTICLE-NA-006`

## Likely Objection: This Is Not A Unicode Proposal

Response: agree. The paper's contribution is a negative readiness audit, not a
proposal. The manuscript must state that the current package is not
proposal-ready.

## Likely Objection: The Matrix Is Too Local

Response: the local scope is intentional. The paper offers a reproducible gap
matrix for deciding what remains missing before any public standards submission
can be considered.

## Likely Objection: Representative Glyphs Are Missing

Response: yes. Representative glyphs are marked blocked because public image
rights and source-region review are missing. This is a result, not an omission
to be hidden.

## Likely Objection: Community Authority Is Not Resolved

Response: yes. The article treats authority as a blocker that cannot be solved
by the technical matrix.

## Revision Rule

Do not answer reviewer pressure by upgrading the claim. Revisions may clarify
the gap matrix, add examples if rights allow, or narrow the scope. They must not
turn this article into a Unicode proposal without new source, rights, standards,
and authority evidence.
