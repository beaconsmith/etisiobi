# Target Venue Fit Review

Article: `ARTICLE-NA-006`

Candidate target venue: `Digital Scholarship in the Humanities (Oxford University Press)`

Status: `TARGET_VENUE_FIT_REVIEW_COMPLETE_HUMAN_SIGNOFF_BLOCKED`

## Venue Basis

Digital Scholarship in the Humanities is a plausible target because this paper
is best framed as a digital-humanities methods contribution: a source-critical
Unicode-readiness gap matrix for an underdocumented African writing-system
artifact.

Official OUP pages rechecked on 2026-07-13:

- https://academic.oup.com/dsh
- https://academic.oup.com/dsh/pages/General_Instructions

The OUP pages describe DSH as an international, peer-reviewed journal for
original digital scholarship in the humanities, with live author guidelines and
formal submission routes. The current instructions state a normal ceiling of
9,000 words for full papers and 5,000 words for short papers; the human venue
decision must choose the appropriate article class before formatting.

The same live instructions require a structured abstract (maximum 250 words),
a data-availability statement, and disclosure of generative or other AI/ML
assistance. They accept LaTeX submissions but request the OUP template and a PDF
for peer review. These are package checks, not evidence that the venue has been
approved by the author.

## Fit Assessment

This article fits DSH only if framed as:

- a digital-humanities method for evaluating script-standardization readiness;
- a source-critical evidence-gap matrix rather than a Unicode proposal;
- a negative readiness result for a culturally significant artifact;
- a reproducible workflow for separating present, partial, blocked, and missing
  evidence requirements.
- a current public standards-status check that strengthens the negative result
  without turning the manuscript into a Unicode proposal.

## Non-Fit Boundaries

The article is not currently a fit if presented as:

- a Unicode proposal;
- a public code chart;
- a complete character repertoire;
- a public glyph-image or manuscript-corpus release;
- a community-authorized standardization action;
- a finished source-critical edition of Nwagu Aneke.

## Required Revisions Before Human Submission

1. Human author approval of Digital Scholarship in the Humanities as the target
   venue.
2. Human source and standards reviewer confirmation that the 12-requirement gap
   matrix is accurate and publicly worded responsibly.
3. Rights/authority approval for the exact text-only package and any
   supplementary material.
4. Final OUP/DSH formatting pass, including author metadata, disclosures,
   permissions, licence, and reference style.
5. Cover letter positioning the manuscript as a readiness-audit method and
   negative result, not as a Unicode proposal.

## Decision

`ARTICLE-NA-006` is a plausible impact-journal submission-review candidate for
Digital Scholarship in the Humanities. It is not externally submission-ready
until human source, rights/authority, author, disclosure, licence/funding, and
final package decisions are recorded.
