# Orthographic Hinge Comparison

Article: `ARTICLE-NA-003`

Status: `COMPARISON_COMPLETE_FOR_SUBMISSION_REVIEW_HUMAN_SOURCE_SIGNOFF_BLOCKED`

## Purpose

The f/v hinge article needs to be evaluated against writing-system
documentation, orthographic modeling, and source-critical digital-humanities
practice. This comparison resolves the non-human positioning gap while keeping
phonological, source-authority, and rights decisions in the human-review lane.

## Comparison Anchors

| Anchor | What it supports | Boundary for ARTICLE-NA-003 |
|---|---|---|
| African writing-system documentation | Nwagu Aneke is a documented writing-system object with public status records and scholarly discussion. | Public status records do not prove that a split f and v row is source-observed. |
| Orthographic modeling | A combined written row can be split for modeling, teaching, normalization, or computational use. | The split is a derived operation, not proof of a new source row or complete phonological review. |
| Unicode/script proposal practice | Standards work needs stable repertoire evidence, names, examples, usage, and community/source review. | The f/v hinge is pre-proposal evidence only; it is not Unicode readiness. |
| TEI, IIIF, and Web Annotation | Digital-humanities infrastructure can record source, transcription, and annotation layers. | These standards do not decide whether f/v is source-observed or derived; the article supplies the local decision rule. |
| Claim verification and citation-grounded generation | Evidence/claim systems can test whether a statement is supported. | A cited claim can still promote a derived operation into a source claim; the hinge rule prevents that local error. |

## What This Comparison Adds

The paper's contribution is not the discovery of f/v, a complete Igbo
phonology, a Unicode repertoire, or a new digital-edition standard. Its
contribution is a bounded hinge rule:

- the source layer keeps the combined f/v row;
- the derived layer may split f and v for explicit modeling purposes;
- the split adds exactly 8 derived cells;
- `27 x 8 = 216` is permitted only when the split operation is labeled.

## Remaining Human Review

The comparison does not complete:

- human review of the f/v row in the source chart;
- human review of any phonological or orthographic interpretation;
- rights clearance for public source-image reproduction;
- cultural/source authority approval for public wording;
- Unicode or standards-body review.

Those remain human sign-off gates. They do not prevent submission-review
candidate status because the proposed article is text-only and derives its
contribution from a reproducible operation boundary.

## Decision

The orthographic hinge comparison is sufficient for bounded submission-review
candidate status if the target venue is asked to evaluate a source/derived
modeling boundary, not a completed phonological account, source edition, or
encoding proposal.
