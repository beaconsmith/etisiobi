# Article-Specific Experiment: EXP-NA-003

## Research Question

Can the f/v distinction be represented as a permitted derived hinge operation
without promoting 27 rows or 216 cells into the primary source-observed Nwagu
Aneke inventory?

## Hypothesis

If the combined source f/v row is transformed into separate f and v derived rows
only under an explicit operation label, then downstream claims can use the split
for modeling while preserving the primary 26 x 8 source foundation.

## Inputs

- `experiments/EXP-NA-003-f-v-hinge-audit/results.json`
- `experiments/EXP-NA-003-f-v-hinge-audit/data/fv_hinge_records.csv`
- `experiments/EXP-NA-003-f-v-hinge-audit/data/fv_hinge_records.jsonl`
- `experiments/EXP-NA-003/results.json`
- `papers/nwagu_aneke_articles/003-f-v-hinge/orthographic_hinge_comparison.md`

## Result

The audit records 8 source-observed combined f/v cells and 16 derived split cells.
The delta is 8 cells, which explains the movement from 26 x 8 = 208 to 27 x 8 =
216 only when the f/v split is applied. The result confirms a derived operation,
not a new source row.

## Negative Control

A manuscript, table, prompt, or interface fails the control if it states 27 rows,
216 cells, separate f and v rows, or a complete f/v glyph inventory without
marking the claim as derived.

## Claim Ceiling

The article can support a formal rule for permitted and forbidden f/v split use.
It cannot support public source-image release, complete phonological review,
community authority approval, or Unicode readiness.
