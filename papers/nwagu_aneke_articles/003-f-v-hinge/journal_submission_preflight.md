## Contribution

The paper contributes a bounded source/derived modeling rule for the Nwagu Aneke
f/v hinge. It shows that the split from combined f/v into separate f and v rows
is a derived orthographic operation that adds exactly 8 derived cells, explaining
the movement from 208 to 216 without promoting 216 into the source-observed
inventory.
