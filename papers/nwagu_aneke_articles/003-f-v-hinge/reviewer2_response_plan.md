turning a derived f/v split into a primary source-row claim. The experiment
quantifies the exact transition from 208 to 216 cells and states when each
number is allowed.

Response: Agree in part. The article does not claim a completed phonological
review. Its contribution is an evidence-layer rule for orthographic modeling.

domain review are attached.

## Likely Objection: Existing provenance standards already solve this.

Response: Standards can encode derivation, but they do not decide whether this
local f/v split is a source claim or a derived operation. The paper contributes
the local decision rule and falsification conditions.

## Likely Objection: The rights boundary makes the paper incomplete.

Response: The rights boundary limits the submission claim. It does not erase the
repo-local result. A journal-facing version must keep the source-image and public
release claims out unless rights clearance is attached.
