# Source-Critical and Digital-Edition Comparison

Article: `ARTICLE-NA-001`

Status: `COMPARISON_COMPLETE_FOR_SUBMISSION_REVIEW_HUMAN_SOURCE_SIGNOFF_BLOCKED`

## Purpose

The earlier prior-art audit correctly flagged that a source-ledger article is
not enough unless it is positioned against established source-critical and
digital-edition practice. This comparison resolves that non-human technical
blocker for submission-review status while leaving line-level source review and
rights/authority decisions to human reviewers.

## Comparison Anchors

| Anchor | What it supports | Boundary for ARTICLE-NA-001 |
|---|---|---|
| TEI critical apparatus | TEI models variant readings, witnesses, and apparatus entries for critical editions. | The paper is not a critical edition because the Nwagu Aneke dossier lacks reviewed glyph-level witnesses and source-region coordinates. |
| TEI characters, glyphs, and writing modes | TEI supplies a language for representing nonstandard characters, glyphs, and writing-mode issues. | The paper may describe a row/vowel ledger and glyph-review need; it must not claim completed glyph encoding. |
| IIIF Presentation API 3.0 and Web Annotation model | IIIF organizes canvases and annotations so digital objects and regions can be presented and discussed. | The local dossier has IIIF/TEI direction but lacks rights-cleared public image regions; the paper remains text-only. |
| PROV-O, RO-Crate, and DataCite | These standards package provenance, research objects, and citable data. | They make the ledger reproducible, but do not themselves validate the source transcription or authorize release. |
| FAIR and CARE principles | These frame reuse, stewardship, and collective/community authority. | The paper must keep public release and authority claims blocked until human authority review. |

## What This Comparison Adds

The paper's contribution is not a replacement for TEI, IIIF, Web Annotation,
PROV-O, RO-Crate, FAIR, CARE, or source-critical editorial practice. Its
contribution is narrower: it identifies the current reconstructable object as a
source-critical row/vowel ledger and uncertainty model, then marks the missing
conditions that separate that ledger from a public digital edition.

## Remaining Human Review

The comparison does not complete:

- line-level Azuonye 1992 review;
- direct Ahamefula/Mbah full-text review;
- independent chart-transcription review;
- rights clearance for public source images or manuscript pages;
- cultural/source authority approval for external wording.

Those remain human sign-off gates. They do not prevent submission-review
candidate status because the proposed article is explicitly text-only and
ledger-focused.

## Decision

The source-critical comparison is sufficient for bounded submission-review
candidate status if the target venue is asked to evaluate a documentation model
and uncertainty ledger, not a completed digital edition or source release.
