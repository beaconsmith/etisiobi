# Article-Specific Experiment

Article: `ARTICLE-NA-001`

Experiment: `EXP-NA-001-source-critical-reconstruction`

Status: `EXECUTED_WITH_SOURCE_REVIEW_BLOCKERS`

## Research Question

Can the current local Nwagu Aneke dossier support a source-critical
reconstruction ledger without overclaiming a completed glyph-level corpus?

## Hypothesis

If the local source files, chart transcription, symbol inventory, count
reconciliation, and BMC cache are treated as separate evidence objects, then the
repo can reconstruct a row/vowel source ledger and uncertainty model while
keeping glyph-corpus, public-release, and speculative theory claims blocked.

## Method

The experiment checked 12 source or structured-evidence files and generated a
12-row evidence table. It separated:

- reconstructable source-file ledger;
- source-observed row labels;
- source-observed vowel/modifier columns;
- 208-record row/vowel index;
- derived 27/216 f/v split layer;
- visible logograph leads;
- manuscript-holdings claims requiring external holdings review.

## Result

The experiment records:

- source files checked: 12;
- evidence rows: 12;
- symbol inventory records: 36;
- row labels: 26;
- vowel columns: 8;
- BMC row/vowel index: 208 records;
- all 208 BMC entries still need human review;
- all 208 entries lack source-region coordinates;
- decision: `RECONSTRUCTABLE_AS_SOURCE_LEDGER_NOT_FULL_GLYPH_CORPUS`.

## What This Result Allows

The article can claim that the current local dossier supports a source-critical
reconstruction ledger and uncertainty model for the Nwagu Aneke row/vowel
structure.

## What This Result Does Not Allow

The experiment does not authorize:

- a completed glyph-level corpus claim;
- public source-image release;
- a final Unicode repertoire proposal;
- source-observed 27/216;
- universal-compression, E6, or complete generative-grammar claims;
- claims that the 100+ manuscript-book corpus has been reviewed locally.

## Remaining Blockers

1. Human source review of Azuonye 1992 and the chart transcription.
2. Rights/authority clearance for public source discussion.
3. Human author/venue sign-off that the documentation-ledger contribution is
   field-significant enough for the selected journal.
