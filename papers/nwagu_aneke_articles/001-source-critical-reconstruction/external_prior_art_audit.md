## Article-Specific Contribution Under Audit

The bounded contribution is:

> a source-critical reconstruction ledger showing that the current local Nwagu
> Aneke dossier supports a row/vowel source ledger and uncertainty model, while
> complete glyph-corpus and public-release claims remain out of scope.

The article does not claim that Nwagu Aneke is newly discovered, that a complete
digital edition exists, or that source images may be publicly reproduced.

## Novelty-Risk Assessment

Risk: `MEDIUM`

The article is plausible as a documentation/source-critical paper only if it is
framed as an uncertainty ledger and reconstruction boundary. It is not novel as
a discovery of the script. It becomes useful if reviewers can inspect a clear
ledger of what is reconstructable now and what remains blocked, and if that
ledger is compared explicitly against established digital-edition and
documentation standards.

## Remaining Before External Submission

1. Line-level source review of Azuonye 1992 and the chart transcription.
2. Direct review of Ahamefula/Mbah full text, or explicit exclusion of those
   claims from the external submission.
3. Domain expert review from African scripts, digital humanities, documentation
   studies, or textual scholarship.
4. Human author approval of the selected documentation/source-critical venue.
5. Rights/authority approval for exact public wording and any reproduced
   materials.

## Current Decision

The prior-art audit supports submission-review candidate status if the article
is framed as a documentation model and uncertainty ledger, not as a complete
glyph-corpus paper. External submission remains blocked by human source,
rights/authority, author, disclosure, and final package decisions.
