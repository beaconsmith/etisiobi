# Reviewer 2 Response Plan: ARTICLE-NA-005

## Likely Objection: Ten records are too small for a critical edition.

Response: Agree. The article is not a complete critical edition. It is a
standards bridge showing what can be mapped now and what remains blocked before
critical-edition status.

## Likely Objection: There are no pixel coordinates.

Response: Correct. Zero records currently have pixel coordinates. That absence is
the central boundary: TEI and Web Annotation leads can be created, but IIIF
coordinate-level claims require source review and rights clearance.

## Likely Objection: Standards citations do not prove local evidence.

Response: The manuscript should not use TEI, IIIF, or Web Annotation as proof
that the Nwagu Aneke source evidence is complete. Standards provide the mapping
vocabulary; the experiment supplies the local evidence and blocker ledger.

## Likely Objection: The rights boundary prevents publication.

Response: It prevents certain publication claims, especially source-image and
coordinate-level claims. A venue-facing article may still contribute a standards
bridge if it keeps those claims blocked and names the missing human decisions.

## Likely Objection: The mapping is only a prototype.

Response: The mapping is a prototype by design. Its research value is the
explicit separation between declaration leads, selectors, reviewed image
coordinates, and public-release authority.
