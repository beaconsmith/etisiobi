# Target Venue Fit Review

Article: `ARTICLE-NA-005`

Candidate target venue: `ACM Journal on Computing and Cultural Heritage`

Status: `TARGET_VENUE_FIT_REVIEW_COMPLETE_HUMAN_SIGNOFF_BLOCKED`

## Venue Basis

ACM Journal on Computing and Cultural Heritage is a plausible target because
this paper is best framed as cultural-heritage computing infrastructure: a
standards-compatible bridge between TEI glyph declarations, Web Annotation
records, and future IIIF coordinate selectors.

Official ACM pages checked on 2026-07-06:

- https://dl.acm.org/journal/jocch
- https://dl.acm.org/journal/jocch/author-guidelines
- https://dl.acm.org/journal/jocch/policies

The ACM pages identify JOCCH as a venue for computing and cultural heritage,
with author guidelines, ethics policies, and formal submission routes.

## Fit Assessment

This article fits JOCCH only if framed as:

- a cultural-heritage computing standards bridge;
- a partial selector-layer mapping for an unencoded African syllabary;
- a missing-coordinate boundary for future critical-edition work;
- a rights-aware, source-review-aware infrastructure paper.

## Non-Fit Boundaries

The article is not currently a fit if presented as:

- a completed IIIF critical edition;
- a public source-image or glyph-crop release;
- a complete glyph corpus;
- a Unicode proposal;
- reviewed pixel-level source annotation;
- community-authorized publication of culturally sensitive material.

## Required Revisions Before Human Submission

1. Human author approval of ACM JOCCH as the target venue.
2. Human source reviewer confirmation of the 10 mapped records and their public
   wording.
3. Rights/authority approval for any IIIF canvas, source-image, glyph-crop, or
   supplementary-file wording.
4. Final ACM/JOCCH formatting pass, including author metadata, disclosures,
   permissions, licence, and reference style.
5. Cover letter positioning the manuscript as a selector-layer bridge and
   missing-coordinate boundary, not as a completed critical edition.

## Decision

`ARTICLE-NA-005` is a plausible impact-journal submission-review candidate for
ACM Journal on Computing and Cultural Heritage. It is not externally
submission-ready until human source, rights/authority, author, disclosure, and
final package decisions are recorded.
