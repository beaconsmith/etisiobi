awareness of cultural heritage, publishing original papers with unpublished data
and innovative methods concerning heritage science.

## Fit Assessment

This article fits Journal of Cultural Heritage only if framed as:

- a cultural-heritage documentation method;
- a source-critical lead-set audit for future corpus review;
- an uncertainty taxonomy for visible whole-word signs;
- a rights-aware and authority-aware staging method, not a release.

## Non-Fit Boundaries

The article is not currently a fit if presented as:

- a completed Nwagu Aneke logograph corpus;
- a public glyph dataset;
- a source-image or glyph-crop release;
- a lexicographic authority claim;
- a Unicode proposal;
- a semantic interpretation of all whole-word signs.
