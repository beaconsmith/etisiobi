# Reviewer 2 Response Plan: ARTICLE-NA-004

## Likely Objection: Thirty leads are too small for a corpus paper.

Response: Agree. The article is not a corpus paper. It argues that a bounded
lead set can be audited and preserved as a review queue without being promoted
into a complete corpus.

## Likely Objection: Unknown glosses weaken the contribution.

Response: The unknown glosses are part of the contribution because they keep the
article from overstating the source. Five rows remain unknown or uncertain; a
journal-facing version should preserve that count and explain what human review
would change.

## Likely Objection: Whole-word signs may not belong in a syllabary paper.

Response: That is exactly why the paper separates whole-word signs from the
26 x 8 CV count. The lead set is adjacent to the syllabic inventory, not evidence
for changing it.

## Likely Objection: Existing provenance standards already handle uncertainty.

Response: Standards can encode uncertainty, but they do not decide whether this
local set is a corpus, lead set, glyph inventory, or review queue. The article
contributes the local classification and failure conditions.

## Likely Objection: Rights boundaries make the article incomplete.

Response: The rights boundary limits the public claim. It does not erase the
repo-local audit. A journal-facing manuscript must avoid source-image release
and complete-corpus claims unless clearance is attached.
