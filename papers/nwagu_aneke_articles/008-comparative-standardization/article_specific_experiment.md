# Article-Specific Experiment

Article: `ARTICLE-NA-008`

Experiment: `EXP-NA-008-comparative-scripts-matrix`

Status: `SUBSTANTIVE_EXPERIMENT_COMPLETE_HUMAN_REVIEW_BLOCKED`

## Research Question

Can a comparative African-script standardization matrix position Nwagu Aneke by
evidence condition rather than by typological analogy alone?

## Hypothesis

If encoded, proposal-lead, and unencoded scripts are compared by
standardization signal and evidence condition, Nwagu Aneke will remain useful
for comparative analysis while still being blocked from Unicode-readiness,
public-repertoire, and community-authorized standards claims.

## Result

The matrix compares 8 scripts. It records 3 encoded comparators and 5
unencoded or proposal-lead comparators. Nwagu Aneke is positioned as the target
case: unencoded, not on the roadmap in the recorded sources, and
review-blocked.

## Negative Control

The article fails if it uses encoded comparators such as Vai, Bamum, or Mende
Kikakui to imply that Nwagu Aneke is equally mature for standards purposes. It
also fails if it treats Ndebe, New Nsibidi, Kpelle, or Loma proposal or
implementation signals as direct evidence for Nwagu Aneke readiness.

## Claim Ceiling

The experiment supports a comparative evidence-condition paper. It does not
support a Unicode proposal, public code chart, complete repertoire, public
glyph-image release, or community-authorized standards claim.
