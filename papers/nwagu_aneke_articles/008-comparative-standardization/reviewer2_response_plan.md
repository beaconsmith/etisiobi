Article: `ARTICLE-NA-008`

Status: `REVIEWER_RESPONSE_PLAN_COMPLETE`

## Likely Objections

1. **The comparator set is too small.**
   Response: the matrix is intentionally small and auditable. Its claim is not a
   universal typology of African scripts; it is a bounded evidence-condition
   comparison for one target case.
2. **Encoded scripts are not comparable to Nwagu Aneke.**
   Response: the article agrees for standards maturity. Encoded scripts are used
   as contrastive controls, not as evidence that Nwagu Aneke is mature.
3. **The paper is not a Unicode proposal.**
   Response: correct. The contribution is a negative readiness and comparison
   method that blocks premature proposal framing.
4. **The script-status labels could become outdated.**
   Response: human source/standards review remains required before submission,
   and any public package must update the status rows against current sources.
5. **Community authority is unresolved.**
   Response: the paper treats that as an active blocker, not a limitation to
   hide.

## Revision Priority

Prioritize source/standards reviewer corrections, then target-venue framing,
then supplementary data policy.
