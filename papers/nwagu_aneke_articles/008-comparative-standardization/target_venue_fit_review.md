## Venue Basis

Written Language & Literacy is a plausible target because this paper is best
framed as a writing-systems and literacy-in-culture contribution: a comparative
evidence-condition matrix for an underdocumented African script.

Official John Benjamins pages checked on 2026-07-06:

- https://benjamins.com/catalog/wll
- https://benjamins.com/catalog/wll/guidelines

The official pages describe the journal as concerned with linguistic,
psycholinguistic, educational, and sociolinguistic accounts of written language
and literacy, and provide contributor guidelines and online submission
directions.

## Fit Assessment

This article fits Written Language & Literacy only if framed as:

- a comparative writing-systems evidence-condition study;
- a source-critical account of standardization maturity, not a standards
  proposal;
- a method for comparing encoded, proposal-lead, and review-blocked African
  scripts;
- a culturally bounded analysis of written-language documentation and literacy
  infrastructure.

## Non-Fit Boundaries

The article is not currently a fit if presented as:

- a Unicode proposal;
- a complete Nwagu Aneke repertoire;
- a public code chart or representative glyph chart;
- a complete history of African syllabaries;
- a community-authorized publication decision;
- a computational benchmark or NLP system paper.
