# External Prior-Art Audit

Article: `ARTICLE-NA-009`

Status: `PRIOR_ART_AUDIT_COMPLETE_HUMAN_SIGNOFF_BLOCKED`

## Prior-Art Areas Checked

1. **Subword tokenization.** BPE and related subword approaches support
   segmentation baselines for open-vocabulary and lower-resource settings.
2. **Tokenizer evaluation risk.** Tokenization choices affect fertility,
   cross-lingual behavior, and representation, so baseline metrics should not
   be inflated into task-performance claims.
3. **Language-resource evaluation.** Language-resource venues expect
   substantive resource creation, use, or evaluation. This article fits only as
   a bounded baseline/project-note style result unless a downstream task is
   added.
4. **Nwagu Aneke source discipline.** The source-observed 26 by 8 inventory and
   derived f/v split must remain separated in computational conditions.
5. **Rights and corpus limits.** A tokenizer experiment over a local word
   sample does not by itself authorize public corpus release or source-image
   release.

## Novelty Boundary

The article does not claim to invent tokenization or prove that Nwagu Aneke
improves Igbo NLP. Its novelty is a source/derived-layer-preserving tokenizer
baseline with explicit refusal of downstream task claims.

## Human Review Still Required

A human source/NLP reviewer must confirm the tokenization framing, corpus
wording, and public evidence scope before any external submission.
