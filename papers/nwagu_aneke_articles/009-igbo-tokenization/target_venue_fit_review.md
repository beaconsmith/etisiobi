Article: `ARTICLE-NA-009`

## Venue Basis

Language Resources and Evaluation is a plausible target because this paper is
best framed as a language-resource evaluation result: a bounded tokenizer
baseline for Igbo that reports segmentation and coverage metrics while refusing
downstream task claims.

Official Springer pages checked on 2026-07-06:

- https://link.springer.com/journal/10579
- https://link.springer.com/journal/10579/submission-guidelines

The Springer pages describe the journal as devoted to the acquisition, creation,
annotation, use, and evaluation of language resources, and the submission
guidelines ask full-length papers to report original substantive research
results involving language resources. The guidelines also include a
less-resourced languages focus.

## Fit Assessment

This article fits Language Resources and Evaluation only if framed as:

- a bounded tokenizer baseline and language-resource evaluation result;
- a less-resourced-language project-note or baseline study;
- a source/derived-layer-preserving computational experiment;
- a negative result that refuses downstream task improvement claims.

## Non-Fit Boundaries

The article is not currently a fit if presented as:

- a completed NLP system;
- a downstream benchmark result;
- a public corpus release;
- a claim that Nwagu Aneke improves Igbo NLP;
- a proof that the derived f/v layer is source-observed;
- a Unicode proposal or writing-system standardization paper.

## Required Revisions Before Human Submission

1. Human author approval of Language Resources and Evaluation as the target
   venue.
2. Human source/NLP reviewer confirmation of corpus wording, tokenizer
   conditions, and public metric claims.
3. Rights/authority approval for the exact text-only and metric-only package
   and any supplementary files.
4. Final Springer/LRE formatting pass, including author metadata, disclosures,
   permissions, licence, and reference style.
5. Cover letter positioning the manuscript as a bounded baseline or project-note
   style language-resource evaluation result, not as a downstream NLP
   improvement paper.

## Decision

`ARTICLE-NA-009` is a plausible impact-journal submission-review candidate for
Language Resources and Evaluation. It is not externally submission-ready until
