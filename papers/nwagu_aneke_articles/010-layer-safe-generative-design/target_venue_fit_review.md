## Venue Basis

ACM Journal on Computing and Cultural Heritage (JOCCH) is the strongest current
target if this paper is framed as computing support for cultural-heritage
research, not as source release or script standardization.

Official ACM pages checked on 2026-07-06:

- https://dl.acm.org/journal/jocch
- https://dl.acm.org/journal/jocch/author-guidelines
- https://dl.acm.org/journal/jocch/policies

The indexed ACM policy and author-guideline pages identify JOCCH as an
interdisciplinary journal with a broad cultural-heritage computing remit and
ethics requirements for research involving human participants or subjects.

## Fit Assessment

This article fits JOCCH only as a bounded cultural-heritage computing systems
paper:

- It turns a cultural-source documentation risk into an executable
  non-promotion invariant.
- It tests the invariant on a 24-case artifact-derived claim matrix.
- It compares the layer gate against provenance-only, citation-only, and
  no-label baselines.
- It now includes a ten-case transfer/counterexample audit spanning
  digital-edition, annotation, Unicode-process, cultural-heritage ML, and
  rights/authority examples.
- It preserves the human authority boundary: the gate rejects overclaiming but
  cannot authorize public release.

## Non-Fit Boundaries

The paper is not currently a fit if presented as:

- a public Nwagu Aneke source-image or manuscript-page release;
- a public glyph or claim dataset paper;
- a Unicode proposal;
- a new formal type-system paper;
- a general AI-safety benchmark;
- a cultural-authority decision.

## Required Revisions Before Human Submission

1. Human author approval of JOCCH as the target venue.
2. Human author decision on output type: public preprint/methods note, full
   JOCCH article, technical report, or no public output yet.
3. Human source reviewer approval of the public Nwagu Aneke wording.
4. Rights/authority approval for the exact examples and any artifact references.
5. Final ACM/JOCCH formatting pass, including author metadata, disclosures,
   rights, and publication licence decisions.
6. Cover letter that positions the contribution as cultural-heritage computing:
   evidence-layer preservation for artifact-derived generative systems.

## Decision

`ARTICLE-NA-010` is a plausible ACM JOCCH submission-review candidate only under
a cultural-heritage computing methods framing. The transfer/counterexample
audit strengthens the public-methods case, but this branch remains below public
journal-manuscript candidate status until human source, rights, author,
target-output, disclosure, and final package decisions are recorded.
