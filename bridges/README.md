# Bridges — Oroma ↔ Etisiobi

Two directions, kept separate on purpose:

- **Outbound (research → product):** what Etisiobi recommends Oroma build.
  Canonical files: `../ETISIOBI_OROMA_FEED.md`,
  `../research/icegov/PRODUCT_BRIDGE.md`, `../research/icegov/OROMA_OGI_SCHEMA_MAP.md`.
- **Inbound (product → research):** signals Oroma emits that may become research.
  Canonical file: [`oroma-signal-ingest.md`](oroma-signal-ingest.md).

Rule: research output is evidence-backed and reviewed before it reaches the
product; product signals are anonymized and never carry raw community data.
