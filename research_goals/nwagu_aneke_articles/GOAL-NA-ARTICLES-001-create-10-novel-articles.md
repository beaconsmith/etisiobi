## Success Criteria

- Ten article candidates exist with research question, novelty hypothesis,
  method, first experiment, falsification condition, and blockers.
- Exactly three candidates are active.
- Every candidate derives from the Nwagu Aneke artifact or its direct
  standards/digitization/application boundary.
- No article claims novelty as proven before systematic review.
- No article promotes E6, universal compression, or exact-27 source claims.

## Failure Criteria

- Article candidates depend mainly on generic research infrastructure rather
  than Nwagu Aneke.
- Candidates cannot be traced to source artifacts, external anchors, or
  falsifiable methods.
- The program collapses source-observed and derived layers.
