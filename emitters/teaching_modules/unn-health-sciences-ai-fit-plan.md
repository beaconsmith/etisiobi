# Planned UNN Health Sciences AI workshop · AI-FIT

**Status:** planned programme design; event details and partner approvals pending. This is not a record of delivery, a deployed application, or a research result.

The Beaconsmith Collective is preparing a two-day, in-person *Artificial Intelligence for Academia Productivity* faculty-development workshop with the University of Nigeria Faculty of Health Sciences and Technology. The current design covers Nursing Sciences, Medical Laboratory Sciences, Medical Radiography and Radiological Sciences, Medical Rehabilitation, and Health Administration and Management.

**AI-FIT (AI Faculty Interactive Toolkit)** is Beaconsmith's proposed participant companion. Its first useful experience should help an academic improve an AI instruction, create and verify a real resource, practise responsible use, and keep a 30-day plan. A printable route must work if connectivity or the app fails. It should not be described as deployed until a working release has been verified.

## Planned evidence and delivery

- A short baseline and parallel post-task will test prompting and critical evaluation alongside confidence.
- Each participant will create and review one teaching or academic resource and a brief teach-back activity.
- Certificate eligibility will require meaningful work and verified participation, with wording approved jointly.
- Routine programme feedback will remain separate from optional formal research and its ethics process.
- Scenarios will use fictional or properly de-identified material; public AI tools must not receive identifiable patient details.

The five department pathways are educational examples, not clinical decision-support systems. Exact dates, venue, faculty naming, logos, signatories, data stewardship and research approvals will be added only after confirmation.

## Relationship to earlier delivery

The [August 2026 Faculty of the Social Sciences workshop](unn-social-sciences-ai-workshop-2026.md) is a completed engagement.
