# UNN Faculty of the Social Sciences · AI for Academia Productivity (2026)

**Record type:** publicly sourced engagement history, not an impact evaluation  
**Event:** 26–27 August 2026, Faculty of the Social Sciences Lecture Hall, University of Nigeria, Nsukka  
**Programme:** *Artificial Intelligence for Academia Productivity*; two-day train-the-trainers workshop  
**Partners as reported:** Faculty of the Social Sciences, Design Thinking Center (DTC), The Beaconsmith Collective  
**Source checked:** [Vanguard, 30 August 2026](https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/08/unn-equips-academics-with-ai-skills-to-transform-research-teaching/)

## What happened

Vanguard reports that the workshop attracted **more than 100** academic and senior staff members and postgraduate students. It identifies Engr. Nzube Chikwado Ndiokwelu, founder of Beaconsmith, as lead trainer, and reports DTC's role in convening the collaboration.

Reported practice areas included literature discovery, research development, academic writing, teaching and assessment, postgraduate supervision, and administrative tasks. The report describes practical work on questioning, verifying and improving AI output, alongside privacy, integrity and academic judgement. It also mentions a 30-day action plan.

These are **reported event details**. The article does not supply participant-level pre/post results, artifact-review data, attendance records, independent observation, or evidence that the 30-day plans were implemented. We therefore do not describe the workshop as having demonstrated measurable productivity gains or lasting training effects.

## What this means for the next cohort

The completed Social Sciences engagement provides a concrete delivery precedent. A forthcoming Faculty of Health Sciences and Technology workshop should retain the practical, judgement-led format while adding department-specific educational cases, a short baseline and matched post-task, an explicit resource rubric, and a controlled record of participation and follow-up.

The Health Sciences workshop is a **planned adaptation**, not an outcome of this event. Its dates, programme agreement and authorised claims belong in its own record once confirmed.
